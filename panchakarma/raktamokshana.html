About Raktamokshana

Raktamokshana describes classical bloodletting practices performed only when clinically appropriate by a trained practitioner.

Individualised Assessment

Any proposed intervention requires an individualized assessment, informed consent and sterile technique.

Safety & Precautions

Bleeding disorders, anemia, blood-thinning medicines, infection risk and other medical factors may make this inappropriate. Never attempt it at home.
